Explicitly Assign Health Facility

Choose a health facility (fixed post or outreach site) to explicitly assign to this settlement. Note that by doing so, the entire population of this settlement will be split evenly among the health facilities that have been explicitly assigned. In other words, the automated distance-based catchment calculation will no longer apply. This means that any health facility not explicitly assigned will not cover this settlement and will not appear in the list of health facilities covering it. It is encouraged to use this feature only when strictly necessary. Note that these explicit assignments can be removed later by clicking and in the list are marked by
